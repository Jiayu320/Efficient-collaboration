## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Define original speed as v (meters/day) and total length L (meters)
Let the original speed be \( v \) meters/day and the total length be \( L \) meters.

### 步骤 2: Compute new speed after 1/3 completion: 1.2v meters/day
Given the original speed is \( v \) meters/day, the new speed after using the new equipment is \( 1.2v \) meters/day.

### 步骤 3: Calculate time at original speed for 1/3 tunnel: L/(3v) days
To calculate the time for completing \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel at the original speed, we use the formula:

\[
\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{L/3}{v} = \frac{L}{3v} \text{ days}
\]

### 步骤 4: Compute reduced working hours: 0.8 * 24 hours
Reduced working hours: 0.8 * 24 = 19.2 hours

### 步骤 5: Calculate time at new speed for 2/3 tunnel: (2/3L) / (1.2v) days
To calculate the time at the new speed for the remaining \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel:

1. From the given information, the new speed is \(1.2v\) meters/day.
2. The portion of the tunnel is \(\frac{2}{3}L\).
3. Therefore, the time to complete \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel at the new speed is \(\frac{\frac{2}{3}L}{1.2v} = \frac{2L}{3 \cdot 1.2v}\).

Simplifying, we get:
\[
\frac{2L}{3.6v} = \frac{L}{1.8v}
\]

Thus, the time to complete \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel at the new speed is \(\frac{L}{1.8v}\) days.

### 步骤 6: Set up equation: time1 + time2 = 185 days
Let \( \text{time1} = \frac{L}{3v} \) and \( \text{time2} = \frac{L}{1.8v} \). Then, \( \text{time1} + \text{time2} = 185 \).

### 步骤 7: Solve equation for L
We have the equation:  
\(\frac{L}{3v} + \frac{L}{1.8v} = 185\).

To solve for \(L\), first find a common denominator:

\(\frac{L}{3v} = \frac{L \times 1.8}{5.4v}\)  
\(\frac{L}{1.8v} = \frac{L \times 3}{5.4v}\).

Combine these:

\(\frac{1.8L + 3L}{5.4v} = 185\).

Simplify:  
\(\frac{4.8L}{5.4v} = 185\).

Multiply both sides by \(5.4v\):

\[ 4.8L = 185 \times 5.4v \]

\[ L = \frac{185 \times 5.4v}{4.8} \].

### 步骤 8: Compute original time: 2 * L/v days
First, calculate \(185 \times 5.4 = 999\). Then, \(L = \frac{999v}{4.8} = 208.125v\). The original time to complete the tunnel is \(2 \times \frac{L}{v} = 2 \times 208.125 = 416.25\) days.

## 最终答案
416.25
