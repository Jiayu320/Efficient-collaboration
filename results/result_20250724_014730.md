# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
Define all possible orientations and placements of the L-shaped tile within the 2x5 rectangle.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: What is an L-shaped tile in this context?
An L-shaped tile consists of 4 unit squares forming an L-shape.

### 步骤 2: How many squares does the L-shaped tile occupy?
An L-shaped tile occupies 4 squares.

### 步骤 3: What are the possible orientations of an L-shaped tile?
An L-shaped tile can be oriented in 2 distinct ways:
1. Corners facing left or right.
2. Corners facing up or down.

### 步骤 4: What are the dimensions of the rectangle to place the L-shaped tile?
2x5 rectangle.

### 步骤 5: How many total squares are in the 2x5 rectangle?
10 squares.

### 步骤 6: For each orientation of the L-tile, what are the possible placements in the 2x5 rectangle?
For each orientation:  
1. **Corners Left/Right**:  
   - Left: Place top-left corner of the L-tile in rows 1-2, columns 1-4.  
   - Right: Place top-right corner in rows 1-2, columns 2-5.  

2. **Corners Up/Down**:  
   - Up: Place bottom-left corner in rows 1-4, columns 1-2.  
   - Down: Place top-left corner in rows 1-4, columns 1-2.

### 步骤 7: How many total unique placements are possible?
For each orientation (4 total), we count placements:  
- Corners Left/Right: 4 (row) × 2 (column) = 8 each.  
- Corners Up/Down: 4 (row) × 2 (column) = 8 each.  

Total: \( 8+8+8+8=32 \) unique placements.

## 最终答案
32
