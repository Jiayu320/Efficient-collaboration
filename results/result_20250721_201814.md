## 原始问题
The graph of $f(x)=\frac{2x}{x^2-5x-14}$ has vertical asymptotes $x=a$ and $x=b$, and horizontal asymptote $y=c$.  Find $a+b+c$.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify that vertical asymptotes occur when the denominator equals zero
Solve \(x^2 - 5x - 14 = 0\).

### 步骤 2: Factor denominator x²-5x-14 = (x-7)(x+2) to find vertical asymptotes at x=7 and x=-2
The factorization gives \(x^2 - 5x - 14 = (x-7)(x+2)\).

### 步骤 3: Recognize horizontal asymptote can be found by examining behavior as x→∞
For large \( x \), the term \( \frac{2x}{x^2} = \frac{2}{x} \approx 0 \). Thus, \( y=0 \).

### 步骤 4: Divide numerator and denominator by highest degree term x² to find limit as x→∞
\(\frac{2x/x^2}{(x^2-5x-14)/x^2} \approx \frac{0}{1} = 0\)

### 步骤 5: Calculate horizontal asymptote y=0 from limit
As \(x \to \infty\), \(f(x) \to 0\).

### 步骤 6: Sum a+b+c = 7+(-2)+0 = 5
The answer is 5.

## 最终答案
5
