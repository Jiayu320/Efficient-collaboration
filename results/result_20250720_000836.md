## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify known information about tunnel construction phases
Known: 
- $\frac{1}{3}$ tunnel at original speed
- New speed: 120% of original
- New time: 80% of original
- Total days: 185

### 步骤 2: Set up equation for time spent on first 1/3 of tunnel at original speed
Let $t$ be the original time to complete the tunnel. Time for first 1/3: $\frac{t}{3}$.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change with new equipment (120% speed Ã 80% time = 96% of original daily progress)
Effective speed = 1.2 * 0.8 = 0.96.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation for remaining 2/3 of tunnel at modified speed
 Remaining time for 2/3: $0.96 \cdot \frac{2t}{3}$.

### 步骤 5: Form total time equation: first_phase + second_phase = 185 days
First phase: $\frac{t}{3}$. Second phase: $\frac{0.8t}{3} \cdot \frac{5}{4}$.

### 步骤 6: Solve for original speed variable and calculate first phase duration
Let \( t \) be the total time to complete the tunnel at original speed. First phase: \(\frac{t}{3}\). Second phase: \(\frac{0.8t}{3} \cdot \frac{5}{4} = \frac{t}{3.75}\). Total: \(\frac{t}{3} + \frac{t}{3.75} = 185\).

### 步骤 7: Multiply original speed by total tunnel length to find total days needed without equipment change
Total days = \(t\). Days for first phase: \(\frac{t}{3}\). Days for second phase: \(\frac{t}{3.75}\). So, \(t = 185\).

## 最终答案
185
