## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify that the tunnel completion consists of two phases with different speeds
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed
Phase 2: $\frac{2}{3}$ at increased speed

### 步骤 2: Recognize that 1/3 of tunnel was built at original speed, 2/3 at modified speed
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed
Phase 2: $\frac{2}{3}$ at 1.2 times original speed, 0.8 working time.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change: 20% speed increase with 80% time = 96% of original daily progress
Effective speed: 1.2 * 0.8 = 0.96 times original speed

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: Let x be original days needed. Then (x/3) + (2x/3 × 100/96) = 185
Equation: \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{100}{96} = 185 \)

### 步骤 5: Simplify equation by converting to common denominator
Okay, let's simplify the equation:

\[
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{100}{96} = 185
\]

Convert \(\frac{100}{96}\) to \(\frac{25}{24}\):

\[
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{25}{24} = 185
\]

### 步骤 6: Solve for x to find total days at original speed
Multiply and simplify:  
\[\frac{x}{3} + \frac{50x}{72} = 185\]  
Combine terms:  
\[\frac{24x + 50x}{72} = 185\]

### 步骤 7: Verify answer makes sense: original time should be more than 185 days
Solve: \(\frac{74x}{72} = 185\)

Check: \(x > 185\)

\(\frac{74 \times 185}{72} > 185\)

\(\frac{74}{72} > 1\)

\(x > 185\) makes sense.
