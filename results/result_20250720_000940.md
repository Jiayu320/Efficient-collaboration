# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two phases of construction - before and after new equipment
Before new equipment: $\frac{1}{3}$ tunnel, After new equipment: $\frac{2}{3}$ tunnel

### 步骤 2: Calculate the portion of tunnel remaining after equipment change (2/3 of total)
Given that $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel was completed after using the new equipment, this is the remaining portion.

### 步骤 3: Analyze speed and time relationship with new equipment (20% faster but 80% working hours means 96% of original daily progress)
New speed = $1.2 \times $ original speed. Working 80% of the time means $0.8 \times $ original speed. So, effective speed = $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ of original speed.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation using time for first third (x/3 days) and remaining two-thirds with modified speed
Let $x$ be the total days to complete at original speed. First third: $\frac{x}{3}$ days. Remaining: $\frac{2x}{3}$ days. At new speed: $\frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{1}{0.96x} = \frac{2x}{2.88x} = \frac{2.5}{3}x$. Total: $\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2.5}{3}x = 185$.

### 步骤 5: Form equation: x/3 + (2x/3)/(0.96) = 185 where x is original total days needed
The equation is correct. Let's solve it:
\[
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185
\]

### 步骤 6: Solve for x by multiplying all terms by 3 and then by 0.96
Multiply by 3: \(x + \frac{6x}{2.88} = 555\). Then by 0.96: \(0.96x + \frac{5.76x}{2.88} = 532.8\).

### 步骤 7: Convert final computation to integer days
Solve: \(0.96x + 2x = 532.8\)
\(2.96x = 532.8\)
\(x = 180\) days
So, the answer is 180 days.

## 最终答案
180
