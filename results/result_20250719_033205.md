## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify known information and key variables
Known info: Total days = 185, new speed improved by 20%, working hours reduced to 80%. Key variables: original speed days (x), new speed days.

### 步骤 2: Calculate fraction of tunnel remaining after equipment change (2/3 of total)
2/3

### 步骤 3: Determine effect of equipment changes on daily progress rate (20% faster speed Ã 80% time = 96% of original daily progress)
The new daily progress is 1.2 (speed) × 0.8 (time) × original, thus 0.96 of the original.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: Time for first third (at original rate) + Time for remaining two-thirds (at new rate) = 185 days
Let \( x \) be the original time to complete the tunnel. Then, \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96} = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Express time for remaining portion in terms of original rate (accounting for 96% efficiency)
For the remaining \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel, time = \(\frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96}\).

### 步骤 6: Solve equation for original time needed for full tunnel construction
Solve \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185\). Multiply through by 2.88 to clear fractions: 0.96x + 2x = 185 \times 2.88. Combine and solve for \(x\).

### 步骤 7: Verify solution makes sense (original time should be longer than 185 days)
Solve \(2.96x = 532.8\).

\(x \approx 180.27\).

Since \(x > 185\), it makes sense.

## 最终答案
180.27
