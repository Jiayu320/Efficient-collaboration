## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Define original speed as S (meters/day) and original time as T (days) with T = 3L, where L is tunnel length
Let \( S \) be the original speed (meters/day) and \( T \) be the original time (days) to complete the tunnel. Given \( T = 3L \), where \( L \) is the tunnel length, we need to express \( T \) in terms of \( S \) and \( L \).

Since the entire tunnel length \( L \) is to be covered at speed \( S \), we have:
\[ T = \frac{L}{S} \]
Given \( T = 3L \), it follows:
\[ \frac{L}{S} = 3L \]
\[ S = \frac{1}{3} \] (L / L) or simply \( S = \frac{1}{3} \) (L / L) \(\approx\) 1/3 (L / L) days/meter for simplification.

However, to keep it simple, we directly use:
\[ T = 3L \] meters days relationship.

### 步骤 2: Calculate new speed after equipment: S_new = 1.2 * S
New speed: \( S_{\text{new}} = 1.2S \)

### 步骤 3: Compute new time per meter: t_new = 0.8 * (1/S)
Given the original speed \( S \approx \frac{1}{3} \) meters/day, the new speed with 20% increase is \( 1.2S \). The working hours are reduced to 80%, so the new time per meter is \( t_{\text{new}} = 0.8 \times \frac{1}{1.2S} = \frac{0.8}{1.2S} = \frac{2}{3S} \).

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: (L/3) / S + (2L/3) / S_new = 185
Okay, let's set up the equation:

- For the first \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel at original speed \(S\):
  \[
  \text{Time} = \frac{L/3}{S}
  \]

- For the remaining \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel at new speed \(1.2S\):
  \[
  \text{Time} = \frac{2L/3}{1.2S} = \frac{2L}{3.6S} = \frac{L}{1.8S}
  \]

- Total time:
  \[
  \frac{L/3}{S} + \frac{L}{1.8S} = 185
  \]

### 步骤 5: Substitute S_new into equation to get L = 185 * 3 * S / (1.2 * 0.8)
To substitute \( S_{\text{new}} \) into the equation, start by expressing the total length \( L \) of the tunnel:  

\[ L = 185 \times 3 \times S / (1.2 \times 0.8) \]   

Simplify:  

\[ L = 185 \times 3 \times S / 0.96 \]  
\[ L = 577.5 \times S \]

### 步骤 6: Express original time T = 3L
We have \( T = 3L \). Since \( L = \frac{L}{S} \), then \( T = 3 \frac{L}{S} \). Thus, \( T = 3 \) days/meter.

### 步骤 7: Substitute L into T equation: T = 3 * [185 * 3 * S / (1.2 * 0.8)] / 3
Substitute \( L \) into \( T \) equation:

Given \( L = 577.5 \times S \), substitute into \( T = 3L \):

\[ T = 3 \times 577.5 \times S = 1732.5 \times S \]

### 步骤 8: Simplify T to 185 * (1.2 / 0.8)
First, simplify \( T \) to \( 185 \times \frac{1.2}{0.8} \):

\[ T = 185 \times 1.5 \]

### 步骤 9: Compute final time: 185 * 1.5 = 277.5 days
The simplified equation is \( T = 185 \times 1.5 \). Calculating this gives \( T = 277.5 \) days.

## 最终答案
277.5
