## 原始问题
Find the last three digits of the product of the positive roots of $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2.$

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Simplify the equation to a more manageable form
To simplify the equation \(\sqrt{1995} x^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2\), let's start by handling the terms separately.

1. **Rewrite the expression:** The equation can be rewritten using the property of exponents and logarithms. Start by letting \( a = \sqrt{1995} \), so we have:
   \[
   a \cdot x^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2 
   \]

2. **Use the property of logarithms:** Recall that \( x^{\log_{b} x} = b^{\log_{b} x} \). Therefore, the left side can be re-expressed as:
   \[
   x^{\log_{1995} x} = 1995^{\log_{1995} x} 
   \]

3. **Combine expressions:** Substituting back into the original equation, we get:
   \[
   a \cdot 1995^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2 
   \]
   \[
   \sqrt{1995} \cdot 1995^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2 
   \]

4. **Simplify the equation:** Note that \( x^{\log_{1995} x} = 1995^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2/a \). Hence:
   \[
   x^{\log_{1995} x} = \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}}
   \]

By simplification, the equation \(\sqrt{1995} x^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2\) becomes:
\[
x^{\log_{1995} x} = \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}}
\]

In conclusion, we've restructured the equation \( \sqrt{1995} x^{\log_{1995} x} = x^2 \) to the more manageable form \( x^{\log_{1995} x} = \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}} \).

### 步骤 2: Express the equation in terms of logarithms
To express the provided equation in terms of logarithms, we start with the restructured form from the relied results:

\[ 
x^{\log_{1995} x} = \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}} 
\]

Taking the logarithm of both sides using base 1995:

1. **Logarithm of the left side:**

   \[
   \log_{1995}(x^{\log_{1995} x}) = \log_{1995}\left(\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}}\right)
   \]

   Using the power rule of logarithms:

   \[
   \log_{1995} x \cdot \log_{1995} x = \log_{1995}\left(\frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}}\right)
   \]

2. **Logarithm of the right side:** Use properties of logarithms:

   \[
   \log_{1995}\left(x^2\right) - \log_{1995}(\sqrt{1995}) = 2 \log_{1995} x - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{x^2}{\sqrt{1995}}

Hence, the equation in terms of logarithms is:

\[
(\log_{1995} x)^2 = 2 \log_{1995} x - \frac{1}{2}
\]

The expression for the equation in terms of logarithms is complete.

### 步骤 3: Find the form of the roots
To find the form of the roots, we start with the equation derived from the relied results:

\[
(\log_{1995} x)^2 = 2 \log_{1995} x - \frac{1}{2}
\]

This is a quadratic equation in terms of \(\log_{1995} x\). Let's set \(y = \log_{1995} x\). The equation becomes:

\[
y^2 = 2y - \frac{1}{2}
\]

Rearranging it, we get:

\[
y^2 - 2y + \frac{1}{2} = 0
\]

This is a standard quadratic equation \(ay^2 + by + c = 0\), where \(a = 1\), \(b = -2\), and \(c = \frac{1}{2}\). The quadratic formula gives the roots of this equation:

\[
y = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
\]

Calculating the discriminant:

\[
b^2 - 4ac = (-2)^2 - 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 4 - 2 = 2
\]

Using the quadratic formula:

\[
y = \frac{-(-2) \pm \sqrt{2}}{2 \cdot 1} = \frac{2 \pm \sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

Thus, the solutions for \(y\) are:

\[
y_1 = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \quad y_2 = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

Returning to the original variable:

\[
\log_{1995} x_1 = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \quad \log_{1995} x_2 = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]

Thus, the roots can be expressed in the form: 

\[
x_1 = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}, \quad x_2 = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}
\]

Conclusion: The form of the roots is \(x_1 = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}\) and \(x_2 = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}\).

### 步骤 4: Identify the positive roots
The current step involves identifying the positive roots of the equation previously transformed into a quadratic in terms of \( y = \log_{1995} x \). We obtained the roots of this quadratic equation:

\[

### 步骤 5: Calculate the product of the positive roots
To find the product of the positive roots \( x_1 \) and \( x_2 \), we begin with the given expressions:

\[
x_1 = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}, \quad x_2 = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}
\]

The product of these roots is:

\[
x_1 \cdot x_2 = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \cdot 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}
\]

Using the property of exponents that \( a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n} \), we compute:

\[
x_1 \cdot x_2 = 1995^{(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) + (1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})} = 1995^{2}
\]

Thus, the product of the positive roots is \( 1995^2 \).

To find the last three digits of \( 1995^2 \), we compute:

\[
1995^2 = 1995 \times 1995 = 1995 \times (2000 - 5)
\]

Use the identity \((a-b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2\):

\[
1995^2 = 2000^2 - 2 \times 2000 \times 5 + 5^2 = 4000000 - 20000 + 25 = 3980025
\]

The last three digits of \( 3980025 \) are \( 025 \).

**Conclusion:** The last three digits of the product of the positive roots are \( \boxed{025} \).
