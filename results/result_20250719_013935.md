# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two phases of construction and their key differences
The two phases are:
1. $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tunnel at original speed
2. $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel at increased speed (20% faster) and reduced hours (80% of original)

### 步骤 2: Set up an equation based on how $\frac{1}{3}$ of tunnel was built at original speed
Let \( x \) be the total days to complete the tunnel at original speed. Then, \(\frac{1}{3}x\) days were spent at original speed.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed ratio after equipment change (120% speed Ã 80% time = 96% per day)
The increased speed is 120% of original, and reduced time is 80% of original. So, effective speed ratio is $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ or 96% per day.

### 步骤 4: Express remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ of tunnel in terms of new adjusted speed
For the remaining $\frac{2}{3}$, at 96% speed, it takes $\frac{2}{3} \div 0.96 = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{25}{24} = \frac{25}{36}x$ days.

### 步骤 5: Write equation: (original days for $\frac{1}{3}$) + (days for $\frac{2}{3}$ at new rate) = 185
\[
\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{25}{36}x = 185
\]

### 步骤 6: Let x = total days at original speed and solve the equation: $\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{0.96} = 185$
The equation to solve is \(\frac{37}{36}x = 185\), derived from the relied result.  

Solving for \(x\):  
\(x = 185 \times \frac{36}{37}\)  

Since \(185 \div 37 = 5\),  
\(x = 5 \times 36 = 180\).  

Thus, \(x = 180\).

\boxed{180}

## 最终答案
\boxed{180}
