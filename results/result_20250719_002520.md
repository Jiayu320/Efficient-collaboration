# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand that the tunnel completion happened in 2 phases with different speeds and times
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tunnel at original speed.
Phase 2: $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel at increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Define initial phase: 1/3 of tunnel at original speed
Let $x$ be the original speed in tunnel/day. Phase 1 takes $\frac{1}{3x}$ days.

### 步骤 3: Define second phase: 2/3 of tunnel at modified speed (20% faster but 80% work hours = 96% of original speed)
Phase 2 speed is $1.2x$ (20% faster). Since working hours are 80%, speed is $1.2x \times 0.8 = 0.96x$.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: Let x be days needed at original speed. Then first phase took (x/3) days and second phase took (2x/3)*(1/0.96) days
First phase: \( \frac{x}{3} \) days. Second phase: \( \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{1}{0.96} \) days. Total: \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96} = 185 \).

### 步骤 5: Create total time equation: (x/3) + (2x/3)*(1/0.96) = 185
The equation is correct. Let's keep it as is.

### 步骤 6: Solve for x by combining terms and multiplying through by 0.96
Given the equation from the problem setup, multiply through by 0.96 to clear the fraction:
\[ 0.96 \left( \frac{1}{3}x + 0.8 \cdot \frac{2}{3}x + \frac{1}{1.2 \cdot 0.8} \cdot \frac{1}{3}x \right) = 185 \cdot 0.96 \]
\[ 0.96 \left( \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{1.6}{3}x + \frac{1}{0.96} \cdot \frac{1}{3}x \right) = 177.6 \]
\[ \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{1.6}{3}x + \frac{1}{0.96} \cdot \frac{1}{3}x = 185 \]

## 最终答案
To solve the given equation, we first simplify and combine like terms:

The equation is:
\[ \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x \cdot 0.8}{3 \cdot 0.96} = 185 \]

Simplify the second term:
\[ \frac{2x \cdot 0.8}{3 \cdot 0.96} = \frac{1.6x}{2.88} = \frac{1.6x}{2.88} = \frac{1.6x}{2.88} = \frac{2x}{3 \cdot 1.2} = \frac{2x}{3.6} = \frac{1.67x}{3} \]

So the equation becomes:
\[ \frac{x}{3} + \frac{1.6x}{3} = 185 \]

Combine the terms:
\[ \frac{x + 1.6x}{3} = 185 \]
\[ \frac{2.6x}{3} = 185 \]

Solve for \( x \):
\[ 2.6x = 185 \times 3 \]
\[ 2.6x = 555 \]
\[ x = \frac{555}{2.6} \]
\[ x = 213.46 \]

Since the number of days must be a whole number and the problem implies the days should be an integer, we round to the nearest whole number, which is 210. Therefore, the total days needed to complete the tunnel at the original speed is:
\[ \boxed{210} \]
