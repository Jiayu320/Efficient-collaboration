## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two speed scenarios and split the tunnel into two parts
The tunnel is split into two parts: the first $\frac{1}{3}$ completed at original speed, and the remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ at the new speed.

### 步骤 2: Calculate that first part (1/3) was built at original speed and remaining 2/3 at modified speed
Let \( t \) be the original time to build the full tunnel. The first \( \frac{1}{3} \) takes \( \frac{t}{3} \). The new speed is \( 1.2 \) times original, and working hours are \( 0.8 \) times original, so effectively \( 0.96 \) times original speed. The remaining \( \frac{2}{3} \) takes \( \frac{2t}{3 \cdot 0.96} \). Total: \( \frac{t}{3} + \frac{2t}{2.88} = 185 \).

### 步骤 3: Set up equation: (1/3 × T)/S + (2/3 × T)/(1.2S × 0.8) = 185, where T is total time at original speed and S is original speed
Equation setup: \(\frac{T}{3S} + \frac{2T}{2.88S} = 185\).

### 步骤 4: Simplify the equation by eliminating S and combining terms with T
Combine the fractions: \(\frac{T}{3S} + \frac{2T}{2.88S} = 185\).

Combine terms: \(\frac{1}{3S} + \frac{1}{1.44S}\).

Simplify: \(\frac{T}{S} \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{1.44}\right) = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Solve the equation for T to find total days needed at original speed
Find the combined fraction: \(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{1.44} = \frac{1.44 + 3}{4.32} = \frac{4.44}{4.32} = \frac{37}{36}\).
