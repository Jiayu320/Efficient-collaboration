# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
Find the last three digits of the product of the positive roots of $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2.$

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the form of the equation and simplify it.
Take log base 1995 on both sides.

### 步骤 2: Solve the simplified equation to find the positive roots.
Let me solve this step:

1) From previous step, we took log base 1995 of both sides:
$\log_{1995}(\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}) = \log_{1995}(x^2)$

2) Using log properties:
$\log_{1995}(\sqrt{1995}) + \log_{1995}(x^{\log_{1995}x}) = 2\log_{1995}x$

3) $\frac{1}{2}\log_{1995}(1995) + (\log_{1995}x)^2 = 2\log_{1995}x$

4) $\frac{1}{2} + (\log_{1995}x)^2 = 2\log_{1995}x$

5) Let y = $\log_{1995}x$, then:
$y^2 - 2y + \frac{1}{2} = 0$

6) Using quadratic formula:
$y = 1 \pm \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{2}} = 1 \pm \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

7) Therefore:
$\log_{1995}x = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ or $\log_{1995}x = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$

8) Thus:
$x = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$ or $x = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$

These are the positive roots.

### 步骤 3: Calculate the product of the positive roots.
Let me find the product of the roots:

Let's call the roots $x_1$ and $x_2$:
$x_1 = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$ and $x_2 = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$

Product = $x_1 \cdot x_2$ 
= $1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \cdot 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$
= $1995^{(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) + (1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})}$
= $1995^2$
= 3980025

Therefore, the product of the positive roots is 3980025.

### 步骤 4: Find the last three digits of the product.
Last three digits: 025

## 最终答案
025
