## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two distinct phases of construction - before and after equipment change
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tunnel at original speed
Phase 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel at increased speed

### 步骤 2: Break down first phase: 1/3 of tunnel at original speed and time
Phase 1: Let $T$ be total days. Original speed completes $\\frac{1}{3T}$ of tunnel/day. So, $\\frac{T}{3}$ days at original speed.

### 步骤 3: Calculate second phase characteristics: 2/3 of tunnel at 120% speed but 80% working hours
Phase 2: New speed is $120\%$ or $1.2$ times original, but working hours are $80\%$ or $0.8$ times. So, effective speed is $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ times original.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation relating total time (185 days) to both phases
Let \( t \) be the total days to complete at original speed. Phase 1: \(\frac{T}{3}\) days. Phase 2: \( \frac{2T}{3} \) days at 0.96 times original speed. Equation: \(\frac{T}{3} + 0.96 \cdot \frac{2T}{3} = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Let x be original total time and express first phase as x/3
Let \( x \) be the total time at original speed. Phase 1: \( \frac{x}{3} \) days.

### 步骤 6: Express second phase time in terms of x and modified rates: (2x/3)*(1/1.2)*(1/0.8)
The second phase time in terms of \( x \) and modified rates is: \(\frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{1}{0.96}\).

### 步骤 7: Form equation: x/3 + (2x/3)*(1/1.2)*(1/0.8) = 185
Combine the phases: \( x/3 + (2x/3)(1/0.96) = 185 \).

### 步骤 8: Solve for x to find original total time
Solve: \( x/3 + (2x/3)(1.0417) = 185 \)  
Simplify: \( x/3 + \frac{2x}{3} \cdot \frac{63}{60} = 185 \)  
Combine: \( x \left( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{63}{60} \right) = 185 \)  
Solve for \( x \): \( x \cdot \frac{1}{3} + x \cdot \frac{126}{180} = 185 \)  
Simplify: \( x \left( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{7}{10} \right) = 185 \)  
Combine: \( x \left( \frac{10 + 21}{30} \right) = 185 \)  
Solve: \( x \cdot \frac{31}{30} = 185 \)  
Finally: \( x = 185 \cdot \frac{30}{31} \)  
Calculate: \( x = 180 \) days

## 最终答案
The total number of days it would have taken to complete the tunnel if they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed is \(\boxed{180}\).
