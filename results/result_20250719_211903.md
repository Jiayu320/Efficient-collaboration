## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two distinct construction periods - before and after equipment change
1. Period 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of tunnel at original speed.  
2. Period 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ with increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Break down the tunnel completion into fractions: 1/3 at original speed, 2/3 at modified speed
1/3 original speed, 2/3 at 1.2x speed.

### 步骤 3: Calculate how the new equipment affected daily progress: 20% speed increase but 80% time means 96% of original daily progress
The new equipment results in a \(1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96\) or 96% of the original daily progress.

### 步骤 4: Let x be the time needed at original speed, then x/3 days were used for first third
\( x/3 \) days were used for the first third of the tunnel.

### 步骤 5: Set up equation: x/3 + (2x/3)/(0.96) = 185, where second term represents remaining work at modified rate
The equation \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96} = 185 \) represents the time taken with improved speed.

### 步骤 6: Solve the equation by multiplying both sides by 96 to eliminate fraction
96 \(\left(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96}\right) = 96 \times 185\).

### 步骤 7: Convert to final answer by isolating x to find total days at original speed
Solve \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185 \) for \( x \).

## 最终答案
