## 原始问题
Find the last three digits of the product of the positive roots of $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2.$

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Let y=log_{1995}x. Rewrite equation as x^y*sqrt(1995)=x^2
Rewrite: \(x^y \cdot \sqrt{1995} = x^2\).

### 步骤 2: Simplify to x^y*1995^(1/2)=x^2
Rewrite: \(x^y \cdot 1995^{1/2} = x^2\).

### 步骤 3: Take log base x: y + (1/2)log_{x}1995 = 2
Take log base x: $y + \frac{1}{2}\log_x{1995} = 2$.

### 步骤 4: Substitute y=log_{1995}x: log_{1995}x + (1/2)log_{x}1995 = 2
Take log base \( 1995 \): \(\log_{1995}x = y\), so \(\frac{1}{2y} = \log_{1995}1995 = 1\). Thus, \(y + \frac{1}{2y} = 2\).

### 步骤 5: Use change of base formula: x=1995 is a root since equation becomes 1+1/2=2
From \( y + \frac{1}{2y} = 2 \), substituting \( y = 1 \) satisfies the equation. Thus, \( \log_{1995}x = 1 \), so \( x = 1995 \).

### 步骤 6: Verify x=1 is another root by direct substitution
Substitute \(x=1\):
\(\sqrt{1995}(1)^{\log_{1995}1}=1^2\)
\(\Rightarrow \sqrt{1995} \cdot 1 = 1\)
This is false, so \(x=1\) is not a root.

### 步骤 7: Find last three digits of 1995*1 = 1995
The only valid root is \( x = 1995 \). The last three digits are 995.

## 最终答案
The last three digits of the product of the positive roots of the equation \(\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2\) are \(\boxed{995}\).
