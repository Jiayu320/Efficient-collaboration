The centers of the small semicircles divide $\overline{AB}$  into four line segments of equal length, as shown. ## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: What is the total length of diameter AB?
Since the three small semicircles are on diameter AB, AB is four times the length of each small semicircle's diameter. The diameter of each small semicircle is 2, so AB is 4*2 = 8.

### 步骤 2: How are the centers of the small semicircles positioned along AB?
The centers are at \( x = -3, 0, 3 \).

### 步骤 3: What is the area of the large semicircle?
The radius of the large semicircle is 4. So, its area is \(\frac{1}{2} \pi (4)^2 = 8\pi\).

### 步骤 4: What is the combined area of the three small semicircles?
Each small semicircle has an area of \(\frac{1}{2}\pi \cdot 1^2 = \frac{\pi}{2}\). So, three semicircles: \(\frac{3\pi}{2}\).

### 步骤 5: What is the area of the shaded region?
Shaded area: \(8\pi - \frac{3\pi}{2} = \frac{13\pi}{2}\).

## 最终答案
The final answer is: $\frac{13\pi}{2}$
