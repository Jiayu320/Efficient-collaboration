# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand that the tunnel was built in two phases with different speeds and times
In Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of tunnel at original speed.
In Phase 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ at increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Identify that first phase completed 1/3 of tunnel at original speed and original hours
First phase took $\frac{1}{3}$ of 185 days.

### 步骤 3: Calculate that second phase completed remaining 2/3 of tunnel with 20% faster speed but 80% working hours
Phase 2 days: $\frac{2}{3} \times 0.8 \times 185 \times \frac{1.2}{1} = 152$ days.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation using 185 total days: Let x be days needed at original speed, then first phase took x/3 days
First phase: $\frac{185}{3}$ days.

### 步骤 5: For second phase, adjust time by combined effect of speed increase and hour reduction: (0.8)/(1.2) = 2/3 multiplier
For the second phase, the combined effect of a 20% speed increase and 20% reduction in working hours results in a time multiplier of \( \frac{0.8}{1.2} = \frac{2}{3} \). This means the time taken for the second phase with the new equipment is \( \frac{2}{3} \) of what it would be at the original speed and hours.

\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}

### 步骤 6: Write complete equation: x/3 + (2x/3)(2/3) = 185, where 2x/3 represents original time for 2/3 of tunnel
The equation is derived from the problem and relied results. Let \( x \) be the total original time in days to complete the tunnel at the original speed.

- First phase time (1/3 of tunnel at original speed): \( \frac{x}{3} \)
- Original time for the remaining 2/3 of tunnel: \( \frac{2x}{3} \)
- With the new equipment, the time multiplier is \( \frac{2}{3} \), so the time for the second phase is \( \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \)
- Total time is 185 days: \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = 185 \)

Thus, the equation is \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} = 185 \).

\boxed{\dfrac{x}{3} + \dfrac{2x}{3} \times \dfrac{2}{3} = 185}

### 步骤 7: Solve the equation for x to find total days needed at original speed
Solve: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{4x}{9} = 185\)

## 最终答案
Let's solve the equation step by step:

\[
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{4x}{9} = 185
\]

First, find a common denominator for the fractions, which is 9:

\[
\frac{3x}{9} + \frac{4x}{9} = 185
\]

Combine the fractions:

\[
\frac{3x + 4x}{9} = 185
\]

Simplify the numerator:

\[
\frac{7x}{9} = 185
\]

To isolate \(x\), multiply both sides by 9:

\[
7x = 185 \times 9
\]

Calculate \(185 \times 9\):

\[
185 \times 9 = 1665
\]

So:

\[
7x = 1665
\]

Divide both sides by 7:

\[
x = \frac{1665}{7}
\]

Calculate the division:

\[
x = 237.85714285714286
\]

Rounding to the nearest whole number (since days are typically whole numbers):

\[
x \approx 238
\]

Thus, the total number of days needed to complete the tunnel at the original speed is:

\boxed{238}
