## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Compute original speed factor: 1 - 0.20 = 0.80
The original equipment's working hours factor after using new equipment is \(0.80\). This means the new equipment allows working \(80\%\) of the original hours. To find the original speed factor compared to the new equipment, we take the reciprocal: \(\frac{1}{0.80} = 1.25\).

So, the original speed is \(1.25\) times the new speed.

### 步骤 2: Calculate original time for 1/3 tunnel: 185 * 3 = 555 days
The original time for 1/3 tunnel is 555 days.

### 步骤 3: Compute new speed factor: 0.80 * 1.20 = 0.96
The new speed is 0.96 times the original speed. So, the original speed is \(\frac{1}{0.96} = \frac{25}{24}\) times the new speed.

### 步骤 4: Compute new time for 1/3 tunnel: 555 / 0.96 = 585.625 days
Given that the new speed is \(0.96\) times the original speed, the old time for \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel is \(555\) days. The original speed is \(\frac{25}{24}\) times the new speed. Therefore, the new time for \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel using the original speed is \(555 \div 0.96 = 585.625\) days. 

To find the total time if they had continued at the original speed:
- Time for \(\frac{1}{3}\) tunnel at original speed: \(585.625\) days.
- Time for \(\frac{2}{3}\) tunnel at original speed: \((185 - 555 \times \frac{3}{2} / 2) \div \frac{25}{24} \times 2 = (185 - 270) \times \frac{24}{25} \times 2 = -85 \times \frac{24}{25} \times 2 = 432\) days.

Total time at original speed: \(585.625 + 432 = 1017.625\) days. Rounding to the nearest whole number, the answer is \(1018\) days.

### 步骤 5: Calculate remaining 2/3 tunnel time: 185 - 585.625 = 127.375 days
185 - 585.625 = 127.375 days

### 步骤 6: Compute total time for 1/3 tunnel at new speed: 127.375 * 0.96 = 122.5 days
From the result, the time to complete 1/3 tunnel at the new speed is 127.375 days. Adjusting for working hours reduction:
\[ 127.375 \times 0.96 = 122.5 \text{ days} \]
So, it takes 122.5 days to complete 1/3 tunnel at the new speed.

### 步骤 7: Sum times for 1/3 and 2/3 tunnels: 585.625 + 122.5 = 708.125 days
Given the steps, we already have the times for 1/3 and 2/3 of the tunnel. Let's correct the sum:

\[ 122.5 + 183 = 305.5 \text{ days} \]

So, it would have taken 305.5 days to complete the tunnel at the original speed.

### 步骤 8: Round final time to one decimal place: 708.1 days
To round 305.5 days to one decimal place, the final answer is 305.5 days.

305.5
