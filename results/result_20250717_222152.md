## 最终答案
To solve the equation \(\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2\), we start by taking the logarithm base 1995 of both sides. Let \(y = \log_{1995}x\), then \(x = 1995^y\). Substituting \(x = 1995^y\) into the equation, we get:
\[
\sqrt{1995}(1995^y)^y = (1995^y)^2
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
\sqrt{1995} \cdot 1995^{y^2} = 1995^{2y}
\]
Since \(\sqrt{1995} = 1995^{1/2}\), we can rewrite the equation as:
\[
1995^{1/2} \cdot 1995^{y^2} = 1995^{2y}
\]
Combining the exponents on the left side, we get:
\[
1995^{1/2 + y^2} = 1995^{2y}
\]
Since the bases are the same, we can equate the exponents:
\[
\frac{1}{2} + y^2 = 2y
\]
Rearranging terms, we get a quadratic equation:
\[
y^2 - 2y + \frac{1}{2} = 0
\]
To solve this quadratic equation, we use the quadratic formula \(y = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\), where \(a = 1\), \(b = -2\), and \(c = \frac{1}{2}\):
\[
y = \frac{2 \pm \sqrt{4 - 2}}{2} = \frac{2 \pm \sqrt{2}}{2} = 1 \pm \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]
Thus, the solutions are:
\[
y = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \quad \text{and} \quad y = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\]
Recall that \(y = \log_{1995}x\), so the positive roots \(x\) are:
\[
x = 1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \quad \text{and} \quad x = 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}
\]
The product of the positive roots is:
\[
1995^{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} \cdot 1995^{1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = 1995^{(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) + (1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})} = 1995^2
\]
We need to find the last three digits of \(1995^2\):
\[
1995^2 = (2000 - 5)^2 = 2000^2 - 2 \cdot 2000 \cdot 5 + 5^2 = 4000000 - 20000 + 25 = 3980025
\]
The last three digits of 3980025 are 025. Therefore, the answer is:
\[
\boxed{025}
\]
