## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify that the tunnel work is divided into two phases with different conditions
First phase: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed.  
Second phase: $\frac{2}{3}$ at increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Establish that Phase 1 covered 1/3 of tunnel at original speed, Phase 2 covered remaining 2/3 with modified conditions
OK, phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed. Phase 2: $\frac{2}{3}$ at 1.2 times speed, 0.8 time hours.

### 步骤 3: Define variables - let x be the number of days taken at original speed, calculate 1/3x for Phase 1
Let \( x \) be the days at original speed. Then \( \frac{1}{3}x \) days for Phase 1.

### 步骤 4: Apply speed and time relationship for Phase 2: New speed = 120%, New time = 80% of original for same work
Phase 2 work in terms of \( x \): \( \frac{2}{3} \times 1.2 \times 0.8x = 0.64x \) days.

### 步骤 5: Set up equation: Phase 1 days + Phase 2 days = 185, with Phase 2 adjusted for speed and time changes
Set up: \( \frac{1}{3}x + 0.64x = 185 \).

### 步骤 6: Solve algebraic equation to find x (original total days)
Solve: \( \frac{1}{3}x + 0.64x = 185 \).

Combine like terms: \( 0.9733x = 185 \).

Divide both sides by 0.9733: \( x \approx 189.99 \).

Thus, \( x = 190 \) days.

### 步骤 7: Verify answer makes sense by checking if Phase 1 and Phase 2 sum to 185 days
Phase 1: \( \frac{1}{3} \times 190 = 63.33 \) days
Phase 2: \( 0.64 \times 190 = 121.6 \) days
Total: \( 63.33 + 121.6 = 184.93 \approx 185 \) days. Verified.

## 最终答案
190
