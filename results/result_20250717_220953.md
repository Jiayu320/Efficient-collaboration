# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
What is the result of 1+1?

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the operation
The operation in the problem \(1 + 1\) is addition.

### 步骤 2: Add the numbers
To complete the task of adding the numbers from the given problem:

\[
1 + 1
\]

We perform the addition operation:

\[
1 + 1 = 2
\]

Conclusion for this step: The result of adding 1 and 1 is 2.

## 最终答案
The final answer is 2.
