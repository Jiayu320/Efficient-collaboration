## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the known variables from the problem
Known variables:
- Total days with new equipment: 185 days
- Original speed increase: 20%
- Working hours reduced to: 80% of original
- Fraction completed before new equipment: \(\frac{1}{3}\)

### 步骤 2: Break down tunnel completion into two phases: before and after equipment change
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tunnel at original speed
Phase 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel at increased speed

### 步骤 3: Calculate time spent on first 1/3 of tunnel at original speed
Let the total time to complete the tunnel at original speed be \(T\) days. The first \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel takes \(\frac{T}{3}\) days. Let \(v\) be the original speed. The time for the remaining \(\frac{2}{3}\) at increased speed:
- Speed: \(1.2v\)
- Time: \(0.8 \times \frac{2T}{3} = \frac{1.6T}{3}\)

Total time: \(\frac{T}{3} + \frac{1.6T}{3} = 185\)

Solve for \(T\):
\[T = \boxed{185 \times \frac{3}{2.6}}\]

### 步骤 4: Set up equation for remaining 2/3 of tunnel with new speed and hours
To solve for \(T\), calculate:
\[T = \frac{185 \times 3}{2.6} = \boxed{210}\]

### 步骤 5: Factor in 20% speed increase and 80% working hours for second phase
To factor in the speed increase and reduced working hours, we first recognize the new effective speed is \(1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96\) times the original speed. This means the second phase completion (2/3 of the tunnel) took \(\frac{1}{0.96}\) of the time it would at original speed. Let \(T\) be the total time at original speed. Given \(\frac{1}{3}T + \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{T}{0.96} = 185\), we solve for \(\frac{1}{3}T + \frac{2T}{2.88} = 185\). Simplifying, we get \(\frac{1}{3}T + \frac{2T}{2.88} = 185\). Combining terms, we find \(T = \boxed{210}\) days.

### 步骤 6: Create algebraic equation using total time of 185 days
To create the algebraic equation using the total time of 185 days:

Let \( T \) be the total time at original speed. The time for \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel is \(\frac{T}{3}\). For the rest \(\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)\) at increased speed by \(1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96\) times the original speed, it is \(\frac{2T}{2.88}\).

The equation is:
\[\frac{T}{3} + \frac{2T}{2.88} = 185.\]

### 步骤 7: Solve for original time per section and multiply by total sections
First, solve the equation \( \frac{T}{3} + \frac{2T}{2.88} = 185 \) for \( T \).

Multiply through by 2.88 to eliminate the fractions:

\[ 0.96T + 2T = 185 \times 2.88. \]

Simplify:

\[ 2.96T = 532.8. \]

Now, solve for \( T \):

\[ T = \frac{532.8}{2.96} \approx 180 \] (using actual division for precision).

Thus, the original total time \( T \) is about 180 days.
