## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand the two-phase nature of construction
First phase: 1/3 at original speed.
Second phase: 2/3 at 1.2 times original speed.

### 步骤 2: Identify that 1/3 of tunnel was built at original speed and 2/3 with new equipment
First phase: 61.67 days.
Second phase: 123 days.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change with new equipment (120% speed × 80% time = 96% of original daily progress)
New speed = 1.2 (original speed) × 0.8 (80% time) = 0.96 (original speed).

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: (1/3)x/original_speed + (2/3)x/(0.96×original_speed) = 185
Equation: \(\frac{1}{3}x/\text{original\_speed} + \frac{2}{3}x/(0.96 \times \text{original\_speed}) = 185\)

### 步骤 5: Simplify equation to standardized form: (1/3)k + (2/3)k/0.96 = 185
Simplify: \( \frac{1}{3}k + \frac{2}{3}k/0.96 = 185 \)

### 步骤 6: Solve for k (original time needed) by combining fractions and isolating k
Combine fractions: \(\frac{k}{3} + \frac{25k}{36} = 185\).

### 步骤 7: Verify answer by checking if both phases sum to 185 days
Solve: \( \frac{k}{3} + \frac{25k}{36} = 185 \)

LCM=36: \( 12k + 25k = 6660 \)

\( 37k = 6660 \)

\( k = 180 \) days

Verify: \( \frac{180}{3} + \frac{25 \cdot 180}{36} = 60 + 125 = 185 \) days

 Correct.

## 最终答案
180
