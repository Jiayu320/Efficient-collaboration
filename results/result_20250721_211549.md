## 原始问题
Find the number of ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of integers such that $|a + bi| \le 5.$

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand that |a + bi| ≤ 5 represents points within or on a circle in the complex plane
The inequality \(|a + bi| \leq 5\) represents a closed disk with radius 5 centered at the origin.

### 步骤 2: Recall that |a + bi| = √(a² + b²) for complex numbers
\(|a + bi| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) for complex numbers.

### 步骤 3: Convert the inequality to a² + b² ≤ 25
Substitute: \(a^2 + b^2 \leq 25\).

### 步骤 4: Recognize we need to count integer coordinates (a,b) inside and on a circle of radius 5
Check integer solutions for \(a^2 + b^2 \leq 25\).

### 步骤 5: Use symmetry: the points will be symmetric about both axes
Consider only \( a, b \geq 0 \) and multiply by 4.

### 步骤 6: Count points in one quadrant (including axis points) and multiply by 4, then adjust for double-counting
Consider \( (a, b) \) for \( a^2 + b^2 \leq 25 \) with \( a, b \geq 0 \). Calculate:  
- \( b = 0: a^2 \leq 25 \) gives \( a \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} \)  
- \( b = 1: a^2 \leq 24 \) gives \( a \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\} \)  
- \( b = 2: a^2 \leq 21 \) gives \( a \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\} \)  
- \( b = 3: a^2 \leq 16 \) gives \( a \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \)  
- \( b = 4: a^2 \leq 9 \) gives \( a \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \)  
- \( b = 5: a^2 \leq 0 \) gives \( a = 0 \)

Number = \(6 + 5 + 5 + 4 + 4 + 1 = 25\).  
Multiply by 4, then adjust.

### 步骤 7: Adjust final count by subtracting duplicated axis points and adding center point
After multiplying by 4, subtract duplicated axis points (5). Add center point (1).
