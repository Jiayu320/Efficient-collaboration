## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two distinct phases of construction: before and after equipment change
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ of the tunnel at original speed.
Phase 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ of the tunnel at increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Break down first phase: 1/3 of tunnel at original speed and timing
Let $T$ be total days. For Phase 1: $\frac{T}{3}$ days for $\frac{1}{3}$ of tunnel.

### 步骤 3: Analyze second phase changes: 20% faster speed but 80% working hours
Phase 2 speed is 1.2 times original, but with 0.8 efficiency. Effectively, it's $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ times original speed.

### 步骤 4: Calculate effective speed in second phase: 20% increase Ã 80% time = 96% of original daily progress
Correct. Phase 2 speed is effectively 0.96 times the original speed.

### 步骤 5: Set up equation: (1/3 Ã x)/original_speed + (2/3 Ã x)/(96% Ã original_speed) = 185
Let's set up the equation based on the given information:

1. Phase 1: \(\frac{T}{3}\) days for \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel.
2. Phase 2: \(\frac{2T}{3}\) days for \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the tunnel at 96% speed.

Thus, the equation is:
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{3}T}{\text{original speed}} + \frac{\frac{2}{3}T}{0.96 \times \text{original speed}} = 185
\]

### 步骤 6: Simplify equation to solve for x (original days needed)
Simplify the equation:

\[
\frac{T}{3 \times \text{original speed}} + \frac{2T}{3 \times 0.96 \times \text{original speed}} = 185
\]
