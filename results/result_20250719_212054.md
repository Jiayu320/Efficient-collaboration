## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify key components: initial speed, new speed after equipment change, and time reduction
Original speed: \( S \), new speed: \( 1.2S \), time reduction: \( 0.8 \).

### 步骤 2: Recognize that 1/3 of tunnel was built at original speed, while 2/3 was built at modified speed/hours
1/3 of tunnel: original speed (S), 2/3: 1.2S, 0.8 hours.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change: 20% speed increase with 80% time means overall speed is 96% of original (1.2 Ã 0.8 = 0.96)
New effective speed is \( 1.2S \times 0.8 = 0.96S \).

### 步骤 4: Set up time equation: Let x be original days needed. Then (1/3)x days for first part and (2/3)x/0.96 days for second part equals 185
Total days = \(\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{2}{3}x/0.96 = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Form algebraic equation: (x/3) + (2x/3)/0.96 = 185
Substitute and simplify: \((x/3) + (2x/3 \times \frac{100}{120}) = 185\).

### 步骤 6: Simplify equation to standard form: x/3 + 25x/36 = 185
Combine the fractions: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{25x}{36} = 185\).

### 步骤 7: Solve for x to find total days at original speed
Solve for \( x \):  
\(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{25x}{36} = 185\).  
Multiply by 36:  
\(12x + 25x = 6660\).

## 最终答案
The tunnel would have taken 240 days to complete at the original speed.
