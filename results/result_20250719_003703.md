# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand that the tunnel construction occurred in two phases with different conditions
Phase 1: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed.  
Phase 2: Remaining $\frac{2}{3}$ at 120% speed, 80% time.

### 步骤 2: Identify the first phase: 1/3 of tunnel at original speed
Phase 1: 65 days

### 步骤 3: Identify second phase: 2/3 of tunnel at modified speed (120% speed, 80% hours = 96% effective speed)
Phase 2: $96\%$ effective speed.

### 步骤 4: Let x be the days needed at original speed. Set up equation: (x/3) + (2x/3)/(0.96) = 185
The given equation for the current step is correctly set up as per the problem conditions and the relied results. The variable \(x\) represents the total days needed at the original speed. The equation:

\[
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{\frac{2x}{3}}{0.96} = 185
\]

captures:
- The first term \(\frac{x}{3}\) is the time taken for the first \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the tunnel at the original speed (consistent with Phase 1 reliance).
- The second term accounts for the remaining \(\frac{2x}{3}\) of the tunnel built at \(0.96\) times the original speed (derived from a \(20\%\) speed increase and \(80\%\) working hours, yielding \(0.96\) effective speed, as per Phase 2 reliance).

Thus, the equation is complete for this step.

**Output**: Done (1 token)

### 步骤 5: Solve the equation to find x
Solve: \(x/3 + \frac{2x}{3 \cdot 0.96} = 185\).

## 最终答案
To solve the equation \( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \cdot 0.96} = 185 \):

1. Simplify the second term:
   \[
   \frac{2x}{3 \cdot 0.96} = \frac{2x}{2.88} = \frac{2x}{\frac{72}{25}} = \frac{2x \cdot 25}{72} = \frac{50x}{72} = \frac{25x}{36}
   \]

2. Substitute back into the equation:
   \[
   \frac{x}{3} + \frac{25x}{36} = 185
   \]

3. Find a common denominator and combine the fractions:
   \[
   \frac{12x + 25x}{36} = 185
   \]
   \[
   \frac{37x}{36} = 185
   \]

4. Solve for \(x\):
   \[
   37x = 185 \cdot 36
   \]
   \[
   37x = 6660
   \]
   \[
   x = \frac{6660}{37}
   \]
   \[
   x = 180
   \]

Therefore, the total number of days needed to complete the tunnel at the original speed is \(\boxed{180}\).
