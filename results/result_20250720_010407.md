# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify known information: 1/3 completed at original speed, then speed increased 20% with 80% time for remaining work, total 185 days
Known:  
1/3 completed at original speed  
new speed = 1.2 original speed  
new time = 0.8 original time  
total time = 185 days

### 步骤 2: Let x be the time needed at original speed and set up equation comparing actual vs hypothetical completion
Let \( x \) be the time for the whole tunnel at original speed. Then:
\[ \frac{x}{3} + 0.8 \cdot \frac{2x}{3} \cdot 1.2 = 185 \]

### 步骤 3: Break down actual completion time: first third at original speed, remaining two-thirds at modified speed
Let's simplify: 
\[ \frac{x}{3} + 0.96x = 185 \]

### 步骤 4: Calculate relative time for second part: 80% time but 120% speed means 2/3 takes (0.8/1.2) of original time
The equation to solve is \( \frac{x}{3} + 0.96x = 185 \).  
Combine the terms:  
\( \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{24}{25}x = 185 \)  
Find a common denominator (75):  
\( \frac{25}{75}x + \frac{72}{75}x = \frac{97}{75}x = 185 \)  
Solve for \( x \):  
\( \frac{97}{75}x = 185 \)  
\( x = 185 \times \frac{75}{97} = \frac{13875}{97} \)  

\boxed{\dfrac{13875}{97}}

### 步骤 5: Form equation: (x/3) + (2x/3)(0.8/1.2) = 185
Simplify and solve:
\(\frac{97}{75}x = 185\)

\[x = 185 \times \frac{75}{97}\]

\[x = \frac{13875}{97}\]

\[x \approx 143.01\]

So, it would have taken approximately 143 days.

### 步骤 6: Simplify fraction in equation: (x/3) + (2x/3)(2/3) = 185
Simplify: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = 185\)

### 步骤 7: Solve for x to find total days needed at original speed
Solve: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{4x}{9} = 185\)

Combine terms: \(\frac{3x + 4x}{9} = 185\)

Simplify: \(\frac{7x}{9} = 185\)

## 最终答案
