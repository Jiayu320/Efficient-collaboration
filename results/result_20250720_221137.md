## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Define original speed as S (meters per day) and total length as L.
Define original speed as \( S \) (meters per day) and total length as \( L \).

### 步骤 2: Compute new speed after 1/3 tunnel: S_new = 1.2S.
Given the original speed \( S \), the new speed after using the equipment is \( 1.2S \).

### 步骤 3: Calculate time for first 1/3 tunnel at original speed: T1 = (1/3)L / S.
Given: S (original speed), L (total length).

Time for first 1/3 tunnel at original speed: \( T1 = \frac{\frac{1}{3}L}{S} = \frac{L}{3S} \).

### 步骤 4: Calculate time for second 2/3 tunnel at new speed: T2 = (2/3)L / S_new.
Given the new speed \( S_{\text{new}} = 1.2S \), we calculate the time for the second \( \frac{2}{3} \) of the tunnel:
\[ T_2 = \frac{\frac{2}{3}L}{1.2S} = \frac{2L}{3 \cdot 1.2S} = \frac{2L}{3.6S} = \frac{L}{1.8S} \]

We know \( \frac{1}{3}L / S = \frac{85 - T_2}{1} \) days, where 85 days is the total time excluding \( T_2 \). Solving for \( T_2 \):
\[ T_2 = \frac{L}{1.8S} \]

### 步骤 5: Compute 80% of original time: T80 = T1 * 0.8.
Given \( T1 = \frac{L}{3S} \), to find 80% of \( T1 \):

\[ T80 = T1 \times 0.8 = \frac{L}{3S} \times 0.8 = \frac{0.8L}{3S} \]

### 步骤 6: Set equation: T1 + T80 = 185 and solve for T1.
Given the equations from the results:

\[ T_1 + T_{80} = 185 \]

\[ T_1 + \frac{0.8L}{3S} = 185 \]

And:

\[ T_1 = \frac{L}{3S} \]

Substitute \( T_1 \) into the first equation:

\[ \frac{L}{3S} + \frac{0.8L}{3S} = 185 \]

Combine the terms:

\[ \frac{L + 0.8L}{3S} = 185 \]

\[ \frac{1.8L}{3S} = 185 \]

\[ \frac{L}{1.5S} = 185 \]

Solve for \( T_1 \):

\[ T_1 = \frac{L}{3S} = \frac{185 \cdot 1.5}{1.8} \]

\[ T_1 = 150 \text{ days} \]

### 步骤 7: Calculate original total time: T_total = 2 * T1.
Given \( T_1 = 150 \) days, calculate \( T_{\text{total}} = 2 \times T_1 \).

\[ T_{\text{total}} = 2 \times 150 = 300 \text{ days} \]

## 最终答案
300
