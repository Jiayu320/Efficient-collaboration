## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify that 1/3 of tunnel was built at original speed
Let \( x \) be the total days to complete at original speed.

### 步骤 2: Calculate that 2/3 of tunnel remained for new equipment phase
2/3 of the tunnel remained.

### 步骤 3: Analyze speed and time changes for new equipment: 20% faster speed but 80% working hours means effective speed is 96% of original
New equipment speed is 1.20 of original, but working hours are 0.80 of original. So effective speed is $1.20 \times 0.80 = 0.96$ of original speed.

### 步骤 4: 未知任务
Calculate the effective days for the original without new equipment: \( x = \frac{185}{\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3 \times 0.96}} \).

### 步骤 5: Solve equation for x to find original time needed
Calculate: \( x = \frac{185}{\frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3 \times 0.96}} \)

## 最终答案
To solve the problem, we need to determine how long it would have taken to complete the tunnel if the new equipment had not been used and the entire tunnel was built at the original speed.

Here are the steps in detail:

1. **Identify the fractions and relationships:**
   - The tunnel was divided into three parts. One-third was built at the original speed, and two-thirds were built with the new equipment.

2. **Calculate the proportion of the tunnel built with the new equipment:**
   - The remaining two-thirds of the tunnel was built using the new equipment. 

3. **Analyze the speed and time changes for the new equipment:**
   - The new equipment increased the construction speed by 20%, so the speed is 1.20 times the original speed.
   - However, the working hours were reduced to 80% of the original, so the effective speed became 1.20 * 0.80 = 0.96 times the original speed.

4. **Set up the equation for the effective time spent with the new equipment:**
   - Since the effective speed with the new equipment is 0.96 times the original speed, the time taken to complete two-thirds of the tunnel with the new equipment would be \(\frac{2}{3 \times 0.96} = \frac{2}{2.88} = \frac{25}{36}\) of the original time \(x\).

5. **Write the total time equation:**
   - The total time taken to complete the tunnel is the sum of the time spent at the original speed for one-third of the tunnel and the time spent with the new equipment for the remaining two-thirds.
   - This can be expressed as:
     \[
     \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2}{3 \times 0.96} x = 185
     \]
   - Simplifying the equation:
     \[
     \frac{x}{3} + \frac{25}{36} x = 185
     \]
     \[
     \frac{12x + 25x}{36} = 185
     \]
     \[
     \frac{37x}{36} = 185
     \]
     \[
     37x = 185 \times 36
     \]
     \[
     37x = 6660
     \]
     \[
     x = \frac{6660}{37} = 180
     \]

So, if the construction company had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken \(\boxed{180}\) days to complete the tunnel.
