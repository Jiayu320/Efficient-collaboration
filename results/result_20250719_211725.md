## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two phases of construction: pre-equipment change (1/3 tunnel) and post-equipment change (2/3 tunnel)
Phase 1: 1/3 tunnel (original speed).  
Phase 2: 2/3 tunnel (increased speed).

### 步骤 2: Analyze speed changes: 20% increase means new speed is 1.2 times original, and time is reduced to 0.8 of original hours
Phase 2 speed: 1.2 times original, time is 0.8 times, so effective rate is \(1.5\) times original.

### 步骤 3: Define original speed as reference point and express time taken for first phase (1/3 tunnel at original speed)
Time for first phase = \(\frac{1}{3}\) of total days at original speed.

### 步骤 4: Calculate effective speed for second phase considering both 20% speed increase and 20% time reduction: 1.2 Ã 0.8 = 0.96 relative to original
The effective speed for the second phase is \(1.2 \times \frac{1}{0.8} = 1.5\) times the original speed.

### 步骤 5: Set up equation using 185 total days: (1/3 Ã T) + (2/3 Ã T Ã 0.96/1) = 185, where T is original time
To set up the equation: \((1/3)T + (2/3)T \times 0.8/1.2 = 185\).

### 步骤 6: Solve the equation: T(1/3 + 2/3 Ã 0.96) = 185, simplifying to T(0.333 + 0.64) = 185
Add the terms in the equation: \( T(0.333 + 0.64) = 185 \) becomes \( T(0.973) = 185 \).

### 步骤 7: Calculate final answer by dividing 185 by 0.973 to find original time T
Divide 185 by 0.973 to find \( T \).  
\[ T = \frac{185}{0.973} \approx 190.23 \]  
So, the original time \( T \) is about 190.23 days.
