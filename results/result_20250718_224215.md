## 原始问题
Find the last three digits of the product of the positive roots of $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2.$

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Set \( y = \log_{1995} x \) and simplify the equation to \( y^2 - 2y + \frac{1}{2} = 0 \)
Rewrite as \( (\log_{1995} x)^2 - 2\log_{1995} x + \frac{1}{2} = 0 \).

### 步骤 2: Determine the sum of y-roots: from the quadratic coefficients, \( y_1 + y_2 = 2 \), and the product of x-roots is \( 1995^{y_1+y_2} = 1995^2 \)
Given \( y_1 + y_2 = 2 \), the product of the x-roots is \( 1995^2 \).

### 步骤 3: Compute \( 1995^2 \mod 1000 \): \( 1995 \mod 1000 = 995 \), \( 995^2 = 990025 \), last three digits are 025
The last three digits of \(1995^2\) are 025.

## 最终答案
The last three digits of the product of the positive roots of the equation \(\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2\) are \(\boxed{025}\).
