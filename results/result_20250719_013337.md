# 问题求解最终结果

## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand the problem structure: split into two phases - before and after equipment change
Before: $\frac{1}{3}$ of tunnel at original speed  
After: $\frac{2}{3}$ of tunnel at $1.2$ times speed, $0.8$ times time

### 步骤 2: Identify known components: 1/3 done at original speed, remaining 2/3 with 20% faster speed but 80% time
Known components:
- $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed
- $\frac{2}{3}$ at $1.2$ times speed, $0.8$ times time
- Total days: 185

### 步骤 3: Set up reference equation: Let x be total days needed at original speed
Let \( x \) be the total days needed at the original speed.

### 步骤 4: Express first phase duration: (1/3)x days for first third
Original speed for 1/3 tunnel: \(\frac{x}{3}\) days.

### 步骤 5: Calculate effective speed ratio for second phase: 120% speed Ã 80% time = 96% of original time needed
Effective speed ratio for second phase: $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$ or 96%

### 步骤 6: Express second phase duration: (2/3)x Ã 0.96 days for remaining work
Second phase days: \(\frac{2}{3}x \times 0.96\).

### 步骤 7: Form equation: (1/3)x + (2/3)x Ã 0.96 = 185
Equation: \(\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{2}{3}x \times 0.96 = 185\)

### 步骤 8: Solve for x to find original time needed
Solve: \(\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{16}{25}x = 185\)  
Combine: \(\frac{25x + 48x}{75} = 185\)  
\(\frac{73x}{75} = 185\)  
\(73x = 185 \times 75\)  
\(73x = 13875\)  
\(x = 190\)

## 最终答案
The total days needed to complete the tunnel at the original speed is \(\boxed{190}\).
