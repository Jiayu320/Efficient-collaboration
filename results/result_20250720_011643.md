## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify that the tunnel construction occurred in two phases
First phase: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed. Second phase: $\frac{2}{3}$ at increased speed.

### 步骤 2: Determine the fraction completed at original speed (1/3) and remaining fraction (2/3) with new equipment
Given: $\frac{1}{3}$ at original speed, $\frac{2}{3}$ at $1.2$ times original speed.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change: 20% speed increase but 80% time means overall rate is 96% of original (1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96)
Effective speed was 96% of original.

### 步骤 4: Set up equation: Let x be original days needed. Then (x/3) + (2x/3 × 1/0.96) = 185
Correct equation: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 1.2 \times 0.8} = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Simplify equation by getting common denominator: (0.96x/3 + 2x/3) = 185 × 0.96
First, simplify: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 1.2 \times 0.8} = 185\)

Next: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185\)

### 步骤 6: Solve for x by combining like terms: (2.96x/3) = 185 × 0.96
Simplify further: \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185\)

Multiply through by 2.88: \(0.96x + 2x = 185 \times 2.88\)

Combine like terms: \(2.96x = 185 \times 2.88\)

### 步骤 7: Calculate final answer: x = (185 × 0.96 × 3)/2.96
Solve: x = (185 × 0.96 × 3) / 2.96
