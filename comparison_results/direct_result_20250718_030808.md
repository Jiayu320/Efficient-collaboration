## 原始问题
Find the last three digits of the product of the positive roots of $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2.$

## 解决方案
Let's solve this step by step.

1) Let's first try to simplify the equation
   * We have $\sqrt{1995}x^{\log_{1995}x}=x^2$

2) Let's use properties of logarithms and exponents:
   * $\log_{1995}x$ means $\frac{\ln x}{\ln 1995}$
   * So the equation becomes: $\sqrt{1995}x^{\frac{\ln x}{\ln 1995}}=x^2$

3) Take the natural log of both sides:
   * $\ln(\sqrt{1995}) + \frac{(\ln x)^2}{\ln 1995} = 2\ln x$

4) Let's let $y = \ln x$
   * Then: $\frac{\ln 1995}{2} + \frac{y^2}{\ln 1995} = 2y$
   * $y^2 - 2y\ln 1995 + (\ln 1995)^2/2 = 0$

5) This is a quadratic in y:
   * $y = \ln x$
   * $y = \ln 1995 \pm \sqrt{(\ln 1995)^2 - (\ln 1995)^2/2}$
   * $y = \ln 1995 \pm \ln 1995\sqrt{1/2}$

6) Therefore:
   * $\ln x = \ln 1995 \pm \ln 1995\sqrt{1/2}$
   * $x = 1995^{1 \pm \sqrt{1/2}}$
   * The two roots are $1995^{1 + \sqrt{1/2}}$ and $1995^{1 - \sqrt{1/2}}$

7) The product of the roots is:
   * $1995^{(1 + \sqrt{1/2})}1995^{(1 - \sqrt{1/2})}$
   * $1995^2$
   * $1995 × 1995$
   * $3980025$

8) The last three digits of 3980025 are 025

Therefore, the last three digits of the product of the positive roots are 025.
