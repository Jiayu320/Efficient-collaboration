## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Understand that the tunnel project has two phases: before and after equipment change
Phase 1: \(\frac{1}{3}\) at original speed; Phase 2: \(\frac{2}{3}\) with new speed.

### 步骤 2: Identify that 1/3 of tunnel was built at original speed and 2/3 with new equipment
Phase 1: \( \frac{1}{3} \) completed at original speed. Phase 2: \( \frac{2}{3} \) completed with increased speed.

### 步骤 3: Calculate effective speed change: New speed = Original speed Ã 1.2 Ã 0.8 = 0.96 Ã Original speed
The effective speed change is: New speed = 0.96 × Original speed.

### 步骤 4: Let x be original days needed. Set up equation: (1/3)x + (2/3)x/0.96 = 185
Combining equations, we get: \(\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{2}{3}(x/0.96) = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Solve equation: (1/3)x + (0.6944)x = 185
Combine the terms: \( \frac{1}{3}x + 0.6944x = 1.0277x = 185 \).

### 步骤 6: Simplify: 1.0277x = 185
To simplify: \( x = \frac{185}{1.0277} \).

### 步骤 7: Calculate final answer: x = 180
\( x = \frac{185}{1.0277} \approx 180 \)

## 最终答案
If the construction company had continued at the original speed without using the new equipment, it would have taken 180 days to complete the tunnel.
