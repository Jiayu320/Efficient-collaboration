## 原始问题
3. (6 points) A construction company was building a tunnel. When $\frac{1}{3}$\ of the tunnel was completed at the original speed, they started using new equipment, which increased the construction speed by $20 \%$ and reduced the working hours to $80 \%$ of the original. As a result, it took a total of 185 days to complete the tunnel. If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken $\qquad$ days to complete the tunnel.

## 解决步骤

### 步骤 1: Identify the two phases of construction - before and after equipment change
Phase 1: Original speed, \(\frac{1}{3}\) completed; Phase 2: New speed, \(\frac{2}{3}\) completed.

### 步骤 2: Calculate fraction of tunnel built in each phase: 1/3 before change, 2/3 after
Phase 1: \( \frac{1}{3} \), Phase 2: \( \frac{2}{3} \) of tunnel completed.

### 步骤 3: Determine the effect of equipment changes on daily progress: 20% speed increase but 80% time = 96% daily progress
Original speed is multiplied by 1.2 (20% increase) and work time by 0.8. So, 1.2 * 0.8 = 0.96 of the original daily progress.

### 步骤 4: Let x be original days needed. Set up equation: (1/3)x + (2/3)x(1/0.96) = 185
Combine like terms: \(\frac{1}{3}x + \frac{2}{3x} \frac{1}{0.96} = 185\) becomes \(\frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{3 \times 0.96} = 185\).

### 步骤 5: Simplify equation: x/3 + (2x/3)(1.0417) = 185
\( \frac{x}{3} + \frac{2x}{2.88} = 185 \)

### 步骤 6: Solve for x: x/3 + 0.6945x = 185
Start by solving for \( x \):  
\[ \frac{x}{3} + 0.6945x = 185 \]  
Combine like terms:  
\[ \left(\frac{1}{3} + 0.6945\right)x = 185 \]  
Convert \(\frac{1}{3}\) to decimal:  
\[ 0.3333x + 0.6945x = 185 \]  
\[ 1.0278x = 185 \]  
Divide both sides by 1.0278:  
\[ x = \frac{185}{1.0278} \approx 180 \]

### 步骤 7: Calculate final answer: x = 180 days
To find \( x \), calculate \( \frac{185}{1.0278} \approx 180 \).

## 最终答案
If they had not used the new equipment and continued at the original speed, it would have taken 180 days to complete the tunnel.
