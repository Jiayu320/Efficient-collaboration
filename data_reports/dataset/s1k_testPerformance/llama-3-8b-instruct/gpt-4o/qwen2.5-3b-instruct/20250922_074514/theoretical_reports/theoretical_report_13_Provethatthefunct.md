# 问题 13 的理论性能分析报告

## 问题描述

Prove that the function \[ f(\nu)= \int_1^{\frac{1}{\nu}} \frac{dx}{\sqrt{(x^2-1)(1-\nu^2x^2)}}\]
(where the positive value of the square root is taken) is monotonically decreasing in the interval  $ 0<\nu<1$ . [P. Turan]
